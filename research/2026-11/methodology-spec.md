# QuanTik Methodology Spec v1 (2026-11-25)
Tags: [W] web-verified this pass; [K] author knowledge (verify citations before publishing). Data: daily OHLCV only. Python backend.
Notation: r_t = ln(P_t/P_{t-1}) on adjusted close; t in trading days; 21d ~ 1m, 252d ~ 1y. Adjust for splits/dividends/bonus shares (critical in VN: stock dividends and rights issues create fake gaps). All signals use data through t-1 close, trade at t open/close (lag 1 day).

## 0. Vietnam market microstructure (drives every design choice)
- Retail ~85-90%+ of trading value; ~99% of accounts [W: vietnamnet/vietstock 2025]. SSC roadmap target 70% retail by 2030 [W]. Implication: behavioural anomalies (overreaction, lottery/MAX, attention) are plausibly stronger than in developed markets [K].
- T+2 settlement; KRX trading system live 5 May 2025; same-day/intraday selling under discussion [W]. Foreign ownership caps: decree effective 11 Sep 2025 stops firms setting arbitrary lower caps [W]; foreign net sold >VND136tn in 2025 [W]; foreign ~14-15% of cap [W]. FTSE upgrade to secondary EM reported [W: tuoitre 2026-01]; verify effective date.
- Price limits: HOSE +-7%, HNX +-10%, UPCOM +-15% [K, verify current]. Limits censor returns: ceiling/floor-lock days truncate daily r_t and induce return autocorrelation (delayed price discovery). Handle: flag lock days (|r|>=limit-0.1%, close==high/low), cap-aware features (use 3-day returns for reversal), exclude lock-streak stocks from tradable set (sec 2).
- Costs: sell tax 0.1%, broker fee ~0.15% each side (varies 0.1-0.3%) -> round-trip ~0.4% base; add slippage by liquidity tier: 0.1% (ADV>50bn), 0.3% (10-50bn), 0.6% (3-10bn) [K, assumptions to calibrate]. Lot 100 shares (HOSE; odd lots possible); min tick 10/50/100 VND by price band -> tick-size noise matters for sub-10k stocks.
- Short selling effectively unavailable to retail -> long-only evaluation; long-short spreads are diagnostics only.
- Stale/zero-volume days common in UPCOM: require >=15 of last 20 days traded for any signal.

Evidence key: Global (G) / Vietnam-frontier-Asia EM (V). Strength: A strong, B moderate, C weak/mixed, ? untested here.
Common preprocessing: market-cap and liquidity filters first (sec 2), then per-date cross-sectional winsorize 1/99 pct, rank->z.

## 2. Operational definitions
### 2.1 Tradable universe (gate before any score)
- Listed >=250 trading days; price >=5,000 VND (tick noise, penny junk) [K assumption];
- ADV20 (median of close*vol, 20d) >= 3bn VND for "visible" ranking; >=10bn VND for "strong stock" headline lists; >=1bn for gem candidates but show liquidity badge (calibrate by percentile: keep ADV20 above 25th pct of HOSE median cross-section, then check capacity: 1m VND position < 1% ADV);
- >=90% days traded in last 60d, zero-volume days <=2 of last 20;
- Exclude: ceiling-lock streak >=3 days in last 20 (r>=limit-0.1% & close==high), floor-lock; >=3 limit-up days in 20; 20d return >60% w/o prior trend; ADV20/ADV250 > 5 ("volume explosion").
- Pump-dump score PD = z(MAX5) + z(AV) + z(limit-up count) + z(-R2 of 60d) ; exclude PD in top decile in-universe. UPCOM: apply double threshold ADV>=5bn and cap its weight in any list at 10% of slots [K assumption]; flag HNX/UPCOM penny name patterns. Manipulation pattern test: post-spike mean reversion -> test "ceiling day followed by -5%+ within 10d" frequency per stock as feature (percent >30% => exclude).
- Survivorship: include delisted tickers (SSC/HOSE delisted lists, forced delistings) in history; if unavailable, flag backtest as biased upward and haircut 2-3%/yr [K assumption] — estimate by simulating delisting rates.

### 2.2 "Cổ phiếu khỏe" (strong stock) - testable
S_strong = 0.30*z(RESMOM) + 0.25*z(TQ) + 0.25*z(H52) - 0.20*z(MAX5) (weights = prior; replace with IC-shrunk weights after sec 4). Percentile-ranked within tradable universe by date -> StrengthScore 0-100.
Label "Khỏe" if ALL: StrengthScore >= 80; close > MA50 > MA200 (or VNINDEX regime-neutral variant); H52 >= 0.85; R2_120 >= 0.6 (set at universe 70th pct, not absolute); MAX1 < 0.8*limit (no lock-driven); ADV20 floor; not PD-excluded; 20d return < 40th... cap (not exhausted: rank of 20d return <=95th pct).
Testable claim: forward 20d/60d excess return and hit-rate of "Khỏe" set vs universe, net of costs.
### 2.3 "Hidden gem"
Gem = Khỏe-lite (StrengthScore >= 70 and TrendQuality >= p65) AND tier mid/small (ADV20 rank within 15th-60th pct of tradable universe, or price-based mcap proxy if shares unavailable) AND low-attention (turnover/ADV rank <= p40 AND AV_20 < 1.5 AND not in top 100 by ADV) AND improving (StrengthScore rank change over 20d >= +15 pct pts AND 3 consecutive weekly improvements) AND PD below p80 AND (later) quality screen (ROE>10%, positive CFO, leverage), so 'gem' isn't junk.
Show as: ranked candidates, max 10-20 names, with liquidity badge and a risk note. Testable: forward return vs Khỏe (large) set and vs same-tier universe; attention alpha = gem minus matched non-gem. Gate: must beat matched-tier benchmark with t>2 (Newey-West), net cost, and survive removal of top-5 contributors (anti-lottery check).

## 3. Validation standard (gates before customer display)
Data: daily panel, rebalance weekly or monthly (monthly default for 21d horizon; weekly with 5d overlap -> Newey-West lag >= h-1).
1. Rank IC: IC_t = Spearman(score_{i,t}, fwd_ret_{i,t+h}) cross-section; h in {5,21,63}. Mean IC, ICIR = mean(IC)/sd(IC) (monthly), t_IC = ICIR*sqrt(T). NW t with lags L = floor(4*(T/100)^(2/9)) or h-1.
2. Fama-MacBeth (1973): each t cross-section regress fwd_ret on [z-scores of factors + controls (log ADV, beta, MOM, REV)]; lambda_t; t = mean(lambda)/(sd/sqrt T) with Newey-West.
3. Quantile spreads: deciles (quintiles if N<400); equal-weight long-only top-decile with cost model; compare to VNINDEX and to equal-weight universe; report excess, tracking error, IR, max DD, hit-rate, turnover (one-way per rebalance), breakeven cost. Cost-adjusted return = gross - turnover*(2*c_side + tax_sell*0.5...) ; i.e. per rebalance cost = sum_i |dw_i|*(fee+slip_i) + sell_turnover*0.1%.
4. Multiple testing: Harvey-Liu-Zhu (2016) t>=3.0 hurdle for new factors (Bonferroni/Holm/BHY lines) [K]; ties: for pre-registered known factors t>=2.0 acceptable only with sign+econ prior; composite must exceed t>=3.
5. Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey-Lopez de Prado 2014) [K]: 
   SR* (threshold) = sqrt(V[SR_k]) * ( (1-g)*Phi^{-1}(1-1/N) + g*Phi^{-1}(1-1/(N*e)) ), g = Euler-Mascheroni 0.5772, N = independent trials (cluster correlated trials: N_eff), V[SR_k] cross-trial variance of Sharpe estimates.
   DSR = Phi( (SR_hat - SR*) * sqrt(T-1) / sqrt(1 - g3*SR_hat + ((g4-1)/4)*SR_hat^2) ), SR per-period, g3 skew, g4 (non-excess) kurtosis of returns, T number of return observations. Gate DSR >= 0.95.
6. PBO via CSCV (Bailey-Borwein-Lopez de Prado-Zhu 2017) [K]: matrix M (T x N) of strategy returns, split time into S even blocks (S=16). For each combination of S/2 blocks as IS (C(16,8)=12,870), OOS = rest: n* = argmax IS Sharpe; omega = rank of n* in OOS (1..N) / (N+1); logit lambda = ln(omega/(1-omega)). PBO = fraction of combinations with lambda<=0 (OOS rank below median). Gate PBO <= 0.20 (reject >0.4).
7. Walk-forward: expanding window, min 3y training, refit annually, test next 12m, OOS concatenated. Purged k-fold (Lopez de Prado 2018): k=5, purge training obs whose label window (h days) overlaps test fold; embargo = max(h, 0.01*T) days after test fold. All hyperparams chosen inside folds only.
8. Min samples: >=8 years (>=96 months) and >=2 regimes (incl. 2018 crash, 2020 covid, 2022 bear); >=100 stocks/date in the tradable universe (check; if fewer in early years use only years with N>=150); >=60 monthly OOS observations; leave final 24m as untouched holdout, unlock once.
9. Robustness: sub-period stability (IC>0 in >=70% of calendar years), exclude top 5% by ADV and bottom 5% (size splits), by exchange (HOSE only vs all), lag signals by +1 day, double costs, bootstrap (stationary block bootstrap, 1000 resamples) CI for mean IC > 0 at 95%.
10. Survivorship/look-ahead: point-in-time universe, delisted included, adjusted prices adjust only with info at t (adjustment factors from corporate events as-of date), no use of today's index membership.
PASS GATES (any factor/composite shown to customers): NW t>=3.0 (composite) / 2.0 (single pre-registered); mean rank IC >= 0.02 at h=21 and ICIR >=0.3 monthly; top-quintile net excess vs equal-weight >= +3%/yr and vs VNINDEX >=0 net of costs; OOS Sharpe >= 0.5*IS and DSR>=0.95; PBO<=0.2; monotonic quantiles (Spearman rho of quantile means >=0.7); turnover <= 100%/month one-way for monthly list; max DD no worse than 1.2x VNINDEX. If failing -> label 'descriptive screen, not predictive' in UI; don't call it 'score of expected return'.

## 4. Composite construction
1. Per date: winsorize 1/99 pct; rank-transform to uniform -> inverse-normal (z) (rank-gauss; robust to heavy tails); missing -> 0 after flag (exclude if >2 factors missing).
2. Neutralization: within-sector (ICB L2; exchange-sector from API if available) demean/z: z' = z - sector mean; plus optionally residualize on log ADV and beta by cross-sectional OLS. Sector-neutral rank by default, then also report non-neutral since VN sectors (banks, real estate, securities) drive theme rallies; test both.
3. Weights: (a) Equal-weight on signed z (baseline, hard to beat; DeMiguel et al 2009 analog); (b) IC-weighted: w_k ∝ max(0, rolling 36m mean IC_k)/ sd? ; (c) shrinkage: w = lambda*w_IC + (1-lambda)*1/K, lambda = T/(T+T0), T0=36m; cap any weight <=40%; (d) ridge/elastic net on FM-lambda (alpha cv within purged folds). Select by OOS comparison under DSR accounting; default = EW unless IC-weighted beats by >=0.1 ICIR OOS.
4. Correlation handling: merge factors with |corr|>0.7 (e.g. MAX & IVOL & VOL) by PCA/first principal component or keep one. Use VIF<5.
5. Regime conditioning: regime state R_t = 1[VNINDEX_t > MA200_t] (also test MA100/150, no more than 3 variants), or HMM-filtered bull prob. Options: (i) risk-on exposure scaling (hold cash when R=0) rather than reweight factors; (ii) factor tilt: momentum only in R=1, low-vol in R=0. Test rule: the number of regime variants counts into N for DSR; accept only if improvement in OOS (walk-forward) Sharpe and max DD both, regime split IC (t-test of IC_up - IC_down with NW), sign is consistent in >=2 sub-periods and economic rationale (momentum crashes after bear rebounds: Daniel-Moskowitz 2016 [K]); use few parameters (binary, 200d fixed), don't optimise MA length on full sample. Trend filter lag +1 day; whipsaw guard: 3-day confirmation / 2% band.

## 5. Per-stock Quant report upgrades
- Thresholds: replace fixed numbers with empirical percentiles: stock's metric vs own 3y history percentile and vs cross-sectional percentile; show 'p83 of own history, p64 of market'. Colors by percentile bands (<20, 20-80, >80) only, not magic values. Report n and window.
- HMM (Hamilton 1989; Ang-Bekaert 2002 [K]): features = [r_t, ln realized vol 10d]. Fit on market (VNINDEX) and per stock w/ min 750 obs; expanding-window refit quarterly (never full-sample smoothing for display) ; display filtered probs P(S_t | r_1..t) not smoothed; label alignment: sort states by mean return (or vol) after every refit, align by Hungarian match to previous refit's means; K by BIC with K in {2,3}, choose 3 only if BIC lower by >10 AND each state occupancy>=10% AND min expected duration>=10d; multiple random starts (>=10), regularize covariance; test OOS: log-likelihood and state-conditional forward vol. Show 'regime probability' not 'forecast'.
- GARCH: GJR-GARCH(1,1)-t: s2_t = w + (a + g*1[e_{t-1}<0]) e_{t-1}^2 + b s2_{t-1}; innovations skew-t via `arch` package; fit rolling 1000d, refit monthly; constraints a+b+g/2<1. Under price limits returns censored: use t errors and warn (vol understated near limit lock).
  VaR backtests (95%/99% 1-day, ≥500 OOS days): Kupiec POF LR_uc = -2 ln[ (1-p)^{T-x} p^x ] + 2 ln[ (1-x/T)^{T-x}(x/T)^x ] ~ chi2(1); Christoffersen independence LR_ind = -2 ln[ (1-pi)^{n00+n10} pi^{n01+n11} ] + 2 ln[ (1-pi01)^{n00} pi01^{n01} (1-pi11)^{n10} pi11^{n11} ], LR_cc = LR_uc+LR_ind ~ chi2(2); gate p>0.05 for both + Basel traffic light (green <= 4 exceed per 250 d at 99%). Fail -> switch to FHS or flag 'model not calibrated'; Per stock: report coverage across all stocks; require >= 90% of liquid stocks pass.
- Probabilities (P(up in 20d), P(beat VNINDEX)): replace with calibrated outputs: base model (logistic/GBM on factor z) -> Platt (sigmoid on logit) or isotonic (needs >1000 samples) fitted on calibration fold (time-ordered); metrics: Brier = mean((p-y)^2), Brier skill vs base rate, ECE with 10 equal-mass bins, reliability diagram, log loss. Gate: Brier skill > 0 OOS, ECE <= 0.03, reliability slope within [0.8,1.2]. If cannot, display only base-rate / empirical frequencies ('in 100 similar past setups, 54 rose'), with n and CI (Wilson).
- Price cones: replace Monte Carlo ARIMA with: (a) FHS / bootstrap from standardized GARCH residuals (Barone-Adesi 1999 [K]), and (b) split conformal intervals (Vovk; Romano-Patterson-Candes 2019 CQR / Angelopoulos-Bates tutorial; Xu-Xie 2021 EnbPI for time-series [K]): calibration residual score s_i = |y_i - yhat_i| / sigma_hat_i on last 250-500 OOS points; q = (1-alpha)(1+1/n) empirical quantile; interval = yhat +- q*sigma_hat. Gate: empirical coverage within +-2pp of nominal on rolling OOS, per exchange; report horizons separately (5/10/21d). Use ARIMA only if it beats random walk by Diebold-Mariano at p<0.05 (usually won't): drop ARIMA drift; use zero or momentum-shrunk drift.
- Monte Carlo: simulate from FHS not Gaussian GBM; show 5/50/95 pct cone; limit-aware clip to +-7/10/15% per day.

## 6. Retail portfolio construction
- Inputs: top N=10-20 names (min 8), pass liquidity filters; sector cap 30%; max position 10% default (15% hard cap), min 3%; HOSE lot 100.
- Weights: inverse-vol w_i ∝ 1/sigma_i (60d EWMA sigma, lambda=0.94 or GARCH) with caps iterative redistribution; Alternative risk parity (equal risk contribution) solve min sum (RC_i - RC_j)^2 using Ledoit-Wolf covariance (Ledoit-Wolf 2004 constant-correlation/ scikit-learn LedoitWolf), 250d daily, N<T. Shrink toward EW 20-30% for stability. Rebalance monthly, only trade if |w - w_target|>2.5pp (turnover band) to cut costs.
- Risk: portfolio VaR/ES 95% 1d & 10d via FHS (bootstrap standardized residuals w/ DCC-correlation or simply historic portfolio return series reweighted by current weights, 500-750d, vol-scaled to current vol) or Student-t (df estimated by MLE, 4-6 typical) parametric ES: ES_a = mu - sigma*( f_t(t_a(df)) / a ) * ( (df + t_a(df)^2)/(df-1) ). Backtest as in sec 5. Report 'worst plausible monthly loss', and show liquidity-days-to-exit = position / (0.2*ADV20).
- Vol targeting optional: scale gross exposure by 15%/ann realised vol, cap 1.0 (no leverage), floor 0.5 w/ cash. Stop-rules: no auto-stops without tested value (stops hurt momentum, test).
- Disclosure: backtest shown with costs, 'hypothetical', last 24m OOS separated, benchmark both; no performance promises; legal copy per SSC advisory-license rules (check; VN investment advice requires license) [K].
